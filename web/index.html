The ANSWER button dials the speed-dial number on the Phone tab when idle; use that tab to change it or to place a test call.

Wi-Fi station

Saving restarts the station. If it cannot connect, the open setup AP esp32SipPhone appears at http://192.168.4.1.

SIP account

Settings are persisted to NVS. Reboot the phone after changing the server, account or transport so the SIP UA re-registers with the new values.

Phone & speed dial

When idle, pressing ANSWER (GPIO3) dials this number. Leave it empty to disable speed dial.

The ring tone and the dialpad key tone share the call speaker but each keeps its own volume, separate from the Audio tab level. A key tone of 0 mutes the dialpad.

Call control

These buttons use the same SIP UA as the physical buttons, so a call can be tested without the keypad.

Audio

Noise reduction and AEC are stored as preferences only; the DSP stage is not implemented.

Actions
